# Contributing

Automonique is not yet accepting external code contributions. Issues,
behavioral reports, and design proposals can be discussed, but external patches
wait until contribution and privacy terms are published.

Repository work follows `AGENTS.md`. In particular, keep secrets, customer or
personal data, private transcripts, real infrastructure identifiers, and
unlicensed material out of the tree.

Commits use the truthful identity of their author. Codex commits use
`Automonique Candidate <candidate@automonique.invalid>`; do not add assistant
attribution or co-author trailers.

Before external contributions open, the project will publish terms that give
the owner the rights needed to distribute contributions under the repository's
licence boundary and separate commercial terms.
