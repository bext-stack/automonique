## Objective

Run three representative synthetic units through the minimal lab under the
owner-configured role policy: mechanical change, durable-state transition and
provider/transport boundary. Include a zero-independent-review trial and a
fresh-context-review trial, record both truthfully, correct harness policy and
rerun from the same bases.

## Verification contract

| Check | Expected |
|---|---|
| Representative units | all three risk classes execute from immutable bases |
| Zero-review policy | a zero-review candidate records zero and follows owner acceptance policy |
| Configured review | requested fresh-context findings bind to the exact frozen diff |
| Fix cycle | accepted blocking finding is fixed, rerun and deterministically resolved |
| Repeatability | pre/post-policy runs use the same bases and comparable metrics |
| No authority gain | trials cannot push, merge, administer, release or deploy |

## Forbidden shortcuts

- claiming provider or reviewer independence not present in evidence;
- changing trial bases after seeing results;
- waiving a blocking finding without owner decision;
- using real customer/provider data.

## Completion evidence

Trial manifests, role records, findings/fixes, before/after metrics and authority
denial results.

## Stop conditions

Stop a trial on private data, unbounded resource use, stale base or authority
outside proposal-only mode.
