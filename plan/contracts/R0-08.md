## Objective

Turn `docs/product-plan/reference/feature-parity.md` into a machine-readable
ledger keyed by row, so parity is a queryable record rather than a prose claim.
Every current feature, script and companion surface carries exactly one status
from the closed set, an owner, a fixture reference and an evidence reference,
including the client portal, client publication, audits, live feed,
notifications, targets and operational commands.

The ledger records classification and evidence. It preserves no behavior itself,
captures no fixture, and closes no gate.

## Ledger contract

- Status is a closed vocabulary: `preserve`, `replace`, `isolate` and
  `retire only by decision`. A row that fits none of them is an explicit
  unclassified finding rather than a nearest-match guess.
- Every row carries an owner and a fixture reference. A row with no behavioral
  evidence records `null` with a reason and must not be spelled `preserve`,
  because an unevidenced preservation claim asserts a fidelity nobody measured.
- The 2026-08-09 owner decision reclassifying unpinned rows to `replace` is
  represented as data, not re-derived. The ledger records which rows that
  decision covers and cites it; the generator never silently reclassifies a row
  on its own.
- The generated ledger is drift-checked against the Markdown source in both
  directions, matching the discipline `BOOT-001` established: a row cannot
  disappear from the ledger, and the ledger cannot invent one.
- Counts are measured and reported per status and per evidence state. The
  pinned, partially pinned and unevidenced subsets are named explicitly rather
  than summarized.
- The ledger names no third-party product for comparison and carries no private
  identifier, so it stays publishable under the scrub boundary.

## Verification contract

| Check | Expected |
|---|---|
| Row coverage | every parity row appears exactly once; the named surfaces are all present; per-status counts are measured |
| Status vocabulary | only the four statuses are accepted; an unclassifiable row is reported as a finding rather than coerced |
| Required fields | every row carries owner, fixture and evidence references, or `null` with a reason; no unevidenced row is spelled `preserve` |
| Decision provenance | the owner reclassification is represented as cited data and is never re-derived by the generator |
| Drift check | the ledger re-derives from its Markdown source and fails on divergence in either direction |
| Publication hygiene | no third-party comparison name and no private identifier appears in the generated ledger |
| Quality | the generator and checker run offline in CI with no network access, no secret and no new runtime dependency |

## Compatibility and evidence

Record measured row counts per status and per evidence state, and name the
unevidenced subset explicitly. Report the ledger as evidence for parity
planning without claiming any behavior is preserved. A missing measurement is
`null` with a reason.

## Stop conditions

Stop on a row that fits no status in the closed set, an unevidenced row being
spelled `preserve`, a silently reclassified row, a generator that re-derives an
owner decision instead of citing it, a ledger that cannot be drift-checked, a
third-party comparison name or private identifier entering the generated
output, an estimated count reported as measured, a licence-boundary violation,
or any path outside the lease.
