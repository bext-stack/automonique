## Objective

Turn `docs/product-plan/requirements/external-capability-ledger.md` into a
machine-readable ledger keyed by capability, with specification, track, owner,
ticket and fixture for every row, and schedule a periodic ecosystem capability
review. This is the work the `capability_ledger_fields_missing` counter
measures: 351 of the repository's 457 specification-debt points sit in this one
ledger.

The ledger records coverage and ownership. It implements no capability and
asserts no compatibility with another product.

## Ledger contract

- Every capability row carries a capability name, an Automonique adaptation
  specification, a track, an owner, a ticket and a fixture reference. Track is
  the closed set `core`, `expansion`, `optional` and `research`, plus an
  explicit safety-adapted replacement.
- No row is silently dropped. A capability the product will not adopt is
  recorded with an explicit replacement or refusal and its reason, never
  deleted.
- A row whose owner, ticket or fixture is genuinely undetermined records `null`
  with a reason. The counter must fall because fields were filled, never
  because a row was removed or a placeholder was invented.
- Ticket references resolve to real work-graph item IDs. A ticket naming an
  item that does not exist is a failure, so the ledger and the graph cannot
  drift apart.
- The ledger names no third-party product for comparison in any generated or
  public artifact. It defines Automonique behavior only.
- The periodic review is scheduled as data — cadence, scope and owner — not as
  prose. The schedule is inspectable and carries no calendar or network
  dependency.
- The generated ledger is drift-checked against its Markdown source in both
  directions and fails on divergence.

## Verification contract

| Check | Expected |
|---|---|
| Row coverage | every capability in the source appears exactly once; per-track counts are measured; no row is dropped |
| Required fields | capability, specification, track, owner, ticket and fixture are present, or `null` with a reason; no invented placeholder |
| Track vocabulary | only the closed track set plus an explicit safety-adapted replacement is accepted |
| Ticket resolution | every ticket reference resolves to a real work-graph item; an unresolvable ticket fails |
| Debt movement | `capability_ledger_fields_missing` falls, and the fall is attributable to filled fields rather than removed rows |
| Review schedule | cadence, scope and owner are inspectable data with no calendar or network dependency |
| Publication hygiene | no third-party comparison name and no private identifier appears in the generated ledger |
| Quality | the generator and checker run offline in CI with no network access, no secret and no new runtime dependency |

## Compatibility and evidence

Record the measured row count per track, the before and after value of
`capability_ledger_fields_missing`, and the subsets still carrying `null`
fields with their reasons. Report the ledger as coverage evidence without
claiming any capability is implemented.

## Stop conditions

Stop on a dropped capability row, an invented owner, ticket or fixture, a
ticket that does not resolve to a real work-graph item, a debt fall caused by
removing rows rather than filling fields, a track outside the closed set, a
third-party comparison name or private identifier entering the generated
output, a ledger that cannot be drift-checked, a review schedule that needs a
network or calendar service, a licence-boundary violation, or any path outside
the lease.
