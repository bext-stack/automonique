## Objective

Generate `.automonique/dev/program.yaml` as the implementation-harness view of
every work item, dependency, gate, track, licence, lease and completion state,
and reject drift in either direction from the executable plan.

The generated DAG must not invent scheduling authority or duplicate prose
contracts. It references `plan/contracts/<ID>.md` and preserves unspecified
items as non-runnable nodes.

## Scope

In scope:

- a deterministic generator from `plan/work-graph.toml` and contract metadata;
- schema/version declaration and stable ordering;
- validation of IDs, edges, gates, leases and contract references;
- forward/reverse drift fixtures and CI-compatible commands.

Out of scope: implementing the Rust harness (`R0-19`), changing work-item
objectives to satisfy the generator, or marking any item done.

## Verification contract

| Check | Expected |
|---|---|
| Coverage | all 375 graph nodes appear exactly once |
| Dependencies | every edge and blocking gate byte-semantically matches the source graph |
| Authority | licence and allowed paths match; unspecified items are not runnable |
| Reproducibility | two generations are byte-identical |
| Forward drift | removing a source node/edge causes a named failure |
| Reverse drift | inventing a generated node/edge causes a named failure |
| Plan regression | `plan/check.py --verify` and `plan/selftest.py` still pass |

## Forbidden shortcuts

- hand-maintaining generated YAML;
- dropping blocked or unspecified nodes;
- embedding credentials, prompts or absolute paths;
- weakening `plan/check.py` or changing item status to make generation pass.

## Completion evidence

Generator command, schema, byte-identical rerun, both drift transcripts, node
and edge counts and plan-regression output.

## Stop conditions

Stop if the source graph cannot express a required harness field; report the
schema gap rather than inventing authority in generated output.
